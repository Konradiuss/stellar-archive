**Free Tide** is an association of independent ports in [[Pelagos]] and [[Halcyon]]. Its people crossed the gates to escape concessions and distant assemblies. They share navigational records and emergency supplies, but each settlement keeps its own laws.

## Ports and councils
The floating cities of [[Thalassa]] send delegates to [[Tide Spindle]]. [[Haven]] contributes rescue crews; [[Shoal]] provides sheltered dry docks. Council meetings rotate between ports so that none becomes a permanent capital in practice.

### Passage rights
The Tide refuses exclusive claims on unexplored routes. Its captains trade with the [[Crucible Combine]] and accept help from the [[Solar Concord]], while declining to join either power.

## The silent convoy
A survey ship followed the supply route toward [[Silent Reach]] and returned with an empty navigation log. The Tide now seeks volunteers to compare that gap with the signal recorded at [[Nacre]].
